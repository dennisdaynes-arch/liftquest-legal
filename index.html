Privacy Policy

This app knows what you lift, what you weigh, and what hurts. Here is exactly where that goes.

Last updated 9 November 2026 · Changes: the AI providers are named (Anthropic and OpenAI); Apple Health and subscriptions added for version 1.0.2.

2. What we collect

Only what the app needs to work. There is no analytics SDK in LiftQuest, no advertising identifier, and no tracking of you across other apps or websites.

Your account

  • Email address, and a display name if you set one
  • A user ID we generate

Your training

  • Workouts, exercises, sets, loads, reps and effort ratings
  • Notes you write on sessions or exercises
  • Bodyweight, body measurements, recovery check-ins, and any injuries or limitations you tell the coach about
  • Training history you choose to import from another app or a file

We treat the last two training groups as health data, because that is what they are, even though you typed or imported them yourself.

Things you share

  • Posts, photos you attach to them, comments, and direct messages
  • Your conversation with the coach

How you use the app

  • Which steps you complete: for example finishing onboarding, a plan being shown or started, a workout saved, or how you answered a permission question. These are stored with your account in our own database.
  • We use them, together with your training records, only to count in aggregate how many people get started and keep training. They are never sold or shared, and never used for advertising.

Apple Health (version 1.0.2 and later, only if you turn it on)

  • If you switch on Settings → Apple Health, LiftQuest saves each workout you finish to Apple Health as a strength-training workout: its start and end time, marked as indoor, and an internal reference so the app can remove it again if you delete the session in LiftQuest. Nothing else is written.
  • LiftQuest reads nothing from Apple Health.
  • What is saved in Apple Health stays in Apple Health, under your control in the Health app. It is not sent to us, to RevenueCat or to the AI coach, is never used for advertising or profiling, and is never sold or shared.
  • You can turn it off in Settings, or remove LiftQuest's access in the Health app at any time. Workouts already saved stay in Health until you delete them there.

Subscriptions (version 1.0.2 and later)

  • Purchases are made with your Apple ID and processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Our subscription processor RevenueCat receives your LiftQuest user ID (so the subscription belongs to your account on any phone) and your App Store purchase records: which plan, whether a free trial is running, and when it renews, ends or is refunded. It also receives standard technical details the store needs, such as app version and platform. It does not receive your email, name, training data or messages.
  • We keep a record of those subscription events with your account, so the coach unlocks correctly and so we can count, in aggregate, how many trials become subscriptions.

Diagnostics

  • If the app crashes: the error and where it happened. No training content, no messages, no personal details.

What we do not collect

  • Your location. LiftQuest never asks for location permission and cannot read GPS. If you type a gym's name, that name is something you wrote, not a position we measured.
  • Contacts, browsing history, search history, or advertising identifiers
  • Anything at all for advertising or profiling

3. Where it is stored

Your data is held in our database, hosted by Supabase on servers in the European Union. Every table restricts rows to the account that owns them.

Some things never leave your phone: your app preferences, the rest timer, and the session you are part-way through.

4. The AI coach

The coach is powered by third-party AI providers, currently Anthropic (Claude) and OpenAI. To answer you, we send them your training figures (lifts, loads, reps, effort), the notes you have written and things you have asked the coach to remember, and your measurements and recovery entries.

Because that includes bodyweight, measurements and anything you have told the coach about pain or injury, health-adjacent information about you is processed by an AI provider outside LiftQuest.

We do not send your email address, display name, user ID, photos, posts, comments, direct messages, or the names of the gyms you train at.

Both providers receive this content through their business APIs, whose terms do not allow them to train their models on it. Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, except content its automated safety systems flag, which it may keep longer to enforce its usage policy. OpenAI may keep API inputs and outputs for up to 30 days to run the service and detect abuse. We do not control those windows.

5. Photos in posts

Read this one carefully. Photos you attach to a workout post are served from public storage. Anyone holding the image's direct link can open it without logging in to LiftQuest.

If someone copies a photo's link while your post is visible, that link keeps working after you delete the post or narrow who can see it. Deleting removes the photo from LiftQuest; it cannot recall a link someone already has.

Photos you never attach to a post are not affected.

6. Who can see what

  • Only you: your training log, measurements, recovery entries, notes, and your conversation with the coach
  • People you choose: posts, to the audience you pick each time: everyone, friends, or only you
  • The person you sent it to: direct messages
  • Nobody, automatically: we do not sell your data, do not share it with advertisers, and do not expose it to other users beyond what you choose to post

If you block someone, the block is enforced by the database, not by hiding things on your screen. Neither of you can reach the other's content.

7. Reporting

You can report a post from the menu on it. A report reaches us, not the person who posted. We review it; the post is not automatically hidden, and its author is never told who reported it. Blocking is separate and takes effect immediately.

8. Keeping and deleting

We keep your data until you delete your account. You can do that yourself: Settings → Delete account. It erases your account and everything in it (sessions, sets, measurements, imported history, posts, photos, messages, and your coach's memory) and cannot be undone. Photos already downloaded or linked by other people are outside our reach, as section 5 explains.

If you only want the coach to forget what it knows about you without losing your training history, Settings → Clear the coach's memory does exactly that.

9. Children

LiftQuest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the data we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, or receive a copy of it. The delete path is in the app. For anything else, write to the address in section 1 and we will respond.

11. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and say what changed. Continuing to use LiftQuest after that means you accept the updated policy.
