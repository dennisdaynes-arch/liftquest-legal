Importing from another app

Export your history as a CSV file from Hevy, Strong or another app, then open Settings → Import history in LiftQuest and choose the file. You can review every exercise before anything is saved, and undo an import later.

Pounds or kilograms

Change it any time in Settings → Weight unit. New sets are logged in the unit you pick.

Subscription

Manage or cancel LiftQuest Coach in Settings → Subscription, or in the iPhone Settings app under your Apple ID → Subscriptions. To get a purchase back on a new phone, tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account erases your account and everything in it. It cannot be undone.
